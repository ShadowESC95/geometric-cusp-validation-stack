# External references / computational provenance

1. L. J. Dixon, U. Oktem, S. Paranjape, J. Trnka, Y. Xu, S.-Q. Zhang, *Multi-Loop Negative Geometries*, arXiv:2605.28926, submitted 27 May 2026. The paper gives the four-point loop-Amplituhedron geometry, the momentum-twistor chart, the logarithm integrand, the collective collinear-cut structure, the frozen-loop variable z, the finite functions F^(0)(z), F^(1)(z), and the contour extraction of the cusp anomalous dimension. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28926

2. N. Arkani-Hamed, J. M. Henn, T. Trnka, *Nonperturbative Negative Geometries: Amplitudes at Strong Coupling and the Amplituhedron*, JHEP 03 (2022) 108, arXiv:2112.06956. https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.06956

3. N. Arkani-Hamed, W. Flieger, J. M. Henn, A. Schreiber, J. Trnka, *Coulomb Branch Amplitudes from a Deformed Amplituhedron Geometry*, Phys. Rev. Lett. 132, 211601 (2024), arXiv:2311.10814. https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.10814

4. FEniCSx / UFL documentation: current project documentation lists DOLFINx 0.11.0.post0 and UFL 2026.1.0. https://docs.fenicsproject.org/

5. Firedrake documentation: current development branch documents finite-element PDE solving, PETSc coupling, multigrid, nonlinear preconditioning, MPI and high-order spaces. https://www.firedrakeproject.org/

6. deal.II: finite-element library; version 9.8.0 released 9 August 2026. https://dealii.org/

7. Meep documentation: FDTD electromagnetic simulation with 1D/2D/3D, anisotropic/dispersive materials and MPI. https://meep.readthedocs.io/en/master/

8. FiPy documentation: NIST finite-volume PDE solver for coupled systems. https://pages.nist.gov/fipy/en/latest/

9. COMSOL PDE documentation: equation-based PDE interfaces and coupled dependent variables. https://www.comsol.com/documentation

10. QuTiP 5.4 documentation: unitary, Lindblad, stochastic, Bloch-Redfield and non-Markovian dynamics. https://qutip.readthedocs.io/

11. pySecDec: numerical calculation of dimensionally regulated parameter integrals by sector decomposition. https://github.com/secdec-research/secdec

12. OpenFOAM Foundation: OpenFOAM 14 released 14 July 2026. https://openfoam.org/release/14/

13. L. F. Alday, J. M. Henn, J. Sikorowski, *Higher loop mixed correlators in N=4 SYM*, arXiv:1301.0149. Source of the integral of x^p giving sin(pi p)/(pi p).

14. J. M. Henn, G. P. Korchemsky, B. Mistlberger, *The full four-loop cusp anomalous dimension in N=4 super Yang-Mills and QCD*, arXiv:1911.10174. Alphabet and boundary condition at x = -1.

15. T. Bargheer, C. Bercini, A. Fernandes, V. Goncalves, J. Mann, arXiv:2406.04294. Relates the cusp anomalous dimension to the discontinuity of F across the negative axis; an independent expression for the two-loop function.

The software adapters in this repository are capability mappings, not claims that each package is a direct proof tool for the amplituhedron itself.
